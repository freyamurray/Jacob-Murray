About

Jacob Murray is a photographer and videographer creating considered visual content for clients in the luxury and travel space.

Over the past five years, he has earned the trust of high-end clients who value his discretion, consistency, and precision behind the camera.

Whether it’s a campaign, social content, digital or analogue, it's the same approach every time: the right craft, the right kit, and the creative vision to deliver.
