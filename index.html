About

Jacob Murray is a photographer and videographer, based in Scotland, who creates thoughtful, beautifully crafted visual content for clients, specialising in luxury automotive, commercial and travel.

Over the past five years, he’s built trusted relationships with his clients, who value his calm presence, discretion, reliability, and attention to detail behind the camera.

Whether he’s working on a campaign, social content, digital or analogue photography, Jacob brings the same considered approach every time: skilled craft, the right kit and a clear creative vision to bring each project to life.
